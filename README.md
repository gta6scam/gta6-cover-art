Vice Panels Deluxe is a multi-panel, GTA-poster-style cover maker that runs entirely in your browser: pick a layout (Poster Five, Triptych, Mosaic Six or Duo Split), drop your own photos into the panels — or start from the bundled samples — then crop and position each photo per panel with drag, zoom and fit/fill controls. Finish the poster with a Vice City colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour or Clean), vignette and film grain, and letter it with a bold two-line caption, subtitle and starburst sticker badge in the vendored Anton display type. One click exports a share-ready PNG at 1080x1080, 1080x1350 or 1920x1080 — and it all works fully offline, with your photos never leaving your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
(then open http://localhost:8080)
